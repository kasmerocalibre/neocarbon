Sustainability

Closing the loop on end-of-life tyres.

Each tonne of recovered carbon black displaces a tonne made from fossil oil, and each tyre processed is one removed from stockpiling or open burning. The reactor runs under seal, and process gas is burned back into the plant rather than flared.
