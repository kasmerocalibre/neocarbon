Technology

A continuous, sealed pyrolysis line built for consistent output

Pyrolysis performs only as well as the material entering it. The majority of our process control sits upstream of the reactor — in grading, sizing, wire separation and drying — so every charge presents the same characteristics and output stays inside the same band batch after batch.

Conversion takes place under seal with oxygen excluded and heat applied indirectly. Vapour is condensed into oil, char is milled into carbon black, and process gas is captured for reuse as fuel.

01

Intake

Loads are weighed, sampled and graded, with rims, tubes and contamination removed before acceptance.

02

Preparation

Casings are reduced to uniform chip, with steel extracted magnetically and textile removed by air classification.

03

Conversion

Prepared chip is charged under seal into an indirectly heated reactor operating without oxygen.

04

Recovery

Vapour is condensed into oil, char is milled into carbon black, and process gas is captured for reuse.

05

Dispatch

Finished material is classified, packed and released against a batch record.

Sustainability

Recovery changes the arithmetic on both sides

India generates significantly more end-of-life tyres each year than the country has licensed capacity to process. Each tonne of recovered carbon black displaces a tonne that would otherwise have been manufactured from fossil oil.

Containment is what makes that case hold. The reactor operates under seal, process gas is cleaned and burned back into the plant, and emissions pass through multi-stage treatment before release.

Quality

Standards we work to

Our products are sampled at discharge and tested using the same recognised methods our customers apply to virgin material. Retained samples are held against every batch number, and third party verification is available on request.

About

An Indian materials recovery company built around a single process

Neo Carbon takes end-of-life tyres apart under controlled conditions and returns every recoverable fraction to industrial use. The plant is engineered around that process rather than adapted to it.

We contract at both ends of the loop — with collectors, fleet operators and municipal contractors on feedstock, and with manufacturers on carbon black, process oil and steel. We also support partners developing recovery capacity in their own regions.
